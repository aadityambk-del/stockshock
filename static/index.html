How it works

From search signals to a signed-off buy list

  1. 01
    Ingest

    Bookings, searches, sold-outs, supplier prices and the festival calendar, week by week.

  2. 02
    Signals

    Seasons, Diwali and summer peaks, growth, and early search demand ~6 weeks ahead.

  3. 03
    Forecast

    Gradient-boosted low / likely / high demand, calibrated so ~8 in 10 weeks land inside the range.

  4. 04
    Optimise

    Tests every plan on 60 demand scenarios: max profit within budget and a cap on the worst 5% loss.

  5. 05
    Decide

    BUY, WAIT or SKIP for each line, with the reason. A buyer approves before anything is spent.
